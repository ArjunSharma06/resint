\section{Method}

We introduce \method, a low-rank adaptation scheme for vision
transformers~\cite{dosovitskiy2020}, building on prior
work~\cite{hu2021lora,zhang2023adaptive}. Ratings were collected from a
single-item instrument. We train with a learning rate of 3e-4 and batch
